Frequently Asked Questions

I'm busy running my business. Do I need to create the content myself?

+

Not at all. Choose the level of involvement that suits you. With EditSimple Productions our team handles everything from ideas and scripts to editing, thumbnails, uploading and optimization. All you do is show up and share your expertise. If you'd rather stay hands-on, our Consulting and Course options guide you every step of the way.

Will YouTube work for my industry?

+

If your clients search for answers before they buy, YouTube can work for you. Coaches, consultants, lawyers, doctors, agencies, financial advisors and other service businesses all use video to build trust at scale. We shape your content around the questions your ideal clients are already asking.

How is this different from running ads?

+

Ads stop the moment you stop paying. A YouTube video keeps getting discovered for months and even years after it's published. Over time your channel becomes a library of evergreen content that brings in leads around the clock, so your results compound instead of resetting every month.

When can I expect to see results?

+

Most channels start gaining traction within the first few months of consistent publishing. Growth builds steadily as your video library grows and YouTube learns who to show your content to. We track performance together and refine the strategy so every video works harder than the last.

Do I need expensive equipment or a studio?

+

No. Many of our clients start with a smartphone or a basic camera and a simple setup at home or in the office. We'll recommend affordable gear and filming tips, and our editing makes your videos look polished and professional.

Introduction

EditSimple Editing Services Private Limited ("Edit Simple", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients, visitors, and users of our website and services. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. By using our services or visiting our website, you agree to the practices outlined below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, billing address; company or business details (if applicable); payment details (handled securely via third-party gateways)
  • Project-Related Information: Video/audio files; scripts and content outlines; reference materials; brand guidelines and visual assets
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, and usage statistics collected through cookies and analytics tools for improving website performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Deliver the services you've requested (video editing, scripting, voiceover, etc.)
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process payments and invoices
  • Send service updates or notifications
  • Improve our services and offerings

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Internal team members and freelancers involved in your project
  • Third-party vendors (e.g., voiceover artists, translators, AI tools) strictly for project completion
  • Government authorities when legally required

In case of an NDA, confidentiality terms will override any such sharing.

4. Use of AI Tools

Edit Simple may use AI tools for scripting, voiceovers, content enhancement, and other automation purposes. You may opt out of AI usage by notifying us in writing — this may affect pricing and turnaround times.

5. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Password-protected project folders
  • Secure cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox)
  • Regular backups

Only authorized personnel have access to your project files.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and track anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of your stored data
  • Request deletion of your data (except where required by law or project commitments)
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communication

To exercise these rights, email us at contact@editsimple.in.

8. Retention Period

We retain project files and personal data for up to 12 months after project completion unless:

  • Longer retention is requested by the client
  • A legal obligation requires us to keep records
  • Files are needed for portfolio/showcase purposes (non-confidential)

9. Third-Party Links

Our site or emails may contain links to third-party tools or platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy policies or practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on our website with a revised effective date.
